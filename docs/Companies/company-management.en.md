# Company management

Company management lets you manage several companies within your account. The companies
(instances) you manage can be arranged hierarchically. The company (instance) you
registered with is the top level of the hierarchy; further companies can be arranged and
managed below it across several levels.

!!! tip
    Companies/instances can be arranged hierarchically, allowing you to model your company
    structure 1:1.

You see your companies/structure when you open the search drop-down at the top of the
application. The hierarchy is shown as a tree. Click the desired company to select it.

!!! tip
    The currently selected company is always shown at the top centre (search drop-down).

### Editing a company

The currently selected company can be edited at any time via the menu on the left. Click
the **"Company"** menu entry. The edit form opens, where you can adjust all company data.
Make sure all entries are complete and follow the respective requirements.

## Carano connection

If you are enabled for the Carano connection, you can enter the required data in the
company settings. You need the **Carano identifier** (also known as the **Carano ID**) and
a Carano login.

!!! tip
    If you do not have all the required information, contact your provider or your
    administrator.

## Importing sub-companies from Carano

Clicking **"Import sub-company from Carano"** transfers all subordinate companies assigned
to the currently selected company in the Carano system.

## Managing Carano tenants

In the company search you can see behind each company name whether the company is
synchronised with Carano.
