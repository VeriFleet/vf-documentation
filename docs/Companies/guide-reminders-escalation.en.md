# Guide: Set up reminders and escalation

Everything under [Drivers & checks](company-edit-drivers.md).

1. Choose the **contact channel**: e-mail or SMS (SMS is charged per message).
2. Set the **reminder interval** (1 to 90 days, 7 without a setting). Example 7 days: request on day 0, reminder on day 7,
   urgent reminder on day 14, escalation from day 21. The preview "This is how it works with this setting:" shows the days.
3. Under **Weekly escalation list** choose whether the company gets only its own list ("Own drivers only"), a "Combined list
   with subsidiaries" or "Combined list only".
4. Check the fleet management's **contact e-mail addresses** under [Master data & services](company-edit-master-data.md):
   they receive the list **Fridays at 10 pm**.
5. Optionally switch on the **warning before the driving licence expires** (days before, 0 = no warning) and **bring the
   check forward**. The warning always goes by e-mail.
6. Apply with **"Save"** at the top right.

Adapt the mail texts of the individual stages under [Communication › Mail texts](company-edit-communication.md).
