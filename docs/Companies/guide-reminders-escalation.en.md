# Guide: Set up reminders and escalation

Everything under [Drivers & checks](company-edit-drivers.md).

1. Choose the **contact channel**: email or SMS (SMS is charged per message).
2. Set the **interval between reminders**. Example 7 days: request on day 0, reminder on day 7, urgent reminder on
   day 14, escalation from day 21. The preview "How it works with this setting" shows the days.
3. Under **Weekly escalation list** choose whether the company gets only its own list, a combined list with
   subsidiaries or only the combined list.
4. Check the fleet management's **contact email addresses** under [Master data & services](company-edit-master-data.md):
   they receive the list **Fridays at 10 pm**.
5. Optionally switch on the **warning before licence expiry** (days before) and **bring check forward**.
6. **Save.**

Adapt the email texts of the individual stages under [Communication › Email texts](company-edit-communication.md).
