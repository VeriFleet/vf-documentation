# Guide: Set up a new customer company

1. Select the parent company and click **"Create subsidiary"** at the top right.
2. Under **Master data & services** enter name, address and the **fleet management contact e-mail addresses**
   and save. Only then are the further settings available (2FA, booked services, reminders, mails, automatic review).
3. Check **Booked services** and **2FA** (both can be inherited from the parent company).
4. Under [Drivers & checks](company-edit-drivers.md) check the **check interval** (preset 180 days; it applies to this
   company only and is not inherited), contact channel and reminder interval. For contact channel and reminder interval,
   **"Inherit"** is usually enough.
5. Under [Platform](company-edit-platform.md) choose the **billing** and the [data source of the
   drivers](company-driver-data.md).
6. If needed, set logo and colours under [Appearance](company-edit-appearance.md).
7. If the customer should send from its own mailbox, follow [Own mailbox as sender](guide-own-mailbox.md).
8. Create the drivers ([Create a user](../Users/user-create.md) or [Excel import](../Users/user-excel-import.md)) and
   check the result with a test request to your own test driver.
