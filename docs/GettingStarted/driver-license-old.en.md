# Old driving licences

**Checking paper licences: automated verification of old driving licences**

In a constantly evolving world of technology, old driving licences are getting a modern
upgrade too. Automated checks based on tamper-proof seals introduce improved verification
that maximises protection against misuse.

## Automated checks for older licences

While older licences lack holographic features, they are still part of the automated
check. This is where the innovation of tamper-proof seals, supported by modern
technologies, comes in.

## Tamper-proof seals

Old licences are fitted with special tamper-proof seals that stand out through their
uniqueness and resistance to manipulation. These seals serve not only as visible
authentication features but also integrate technologies enabling automated verification.

## App-based authentication

App-based authentication plays a key role in this control mechanism. The dedicated mobile
application scans the tamper-proof seal on the licence and verifies its authenticity —
fast, precise, and with additional security functions.

## Non-destructive authentication

The tamper-proof seal is designed to prevent misuse. Thanks to special adhesives and
materials, the seal cannot be removed from the licence without destroying it. Any attempt
at manipulation is immediately visible and voids the document's authenticity.

## Benefits for security and efficiency

Automated checking of old licences with tamper-proof seals offers many advantages: higher
security through modern technology and efficient verification.

## Outlook

Automated checks for old driving licences mark significant progress in licence
verification. While we embrace these technologies, the security of personal data and the
protection of privacy remain the top priority.
