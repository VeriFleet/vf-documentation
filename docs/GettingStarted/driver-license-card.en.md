# Card driving licences

**Driving-licence checks meet AI: a glimpse into the future of identification
technologies.**

In a world shaped by continuous technological progress, driving-licence checks are being
revolutionised by artificial intelligence (AI). AI integration enables not only more
efficient licence verification but also the recognition of specific features — including
the identification of holographic elements.

## Automated licence checks with AI

Traditional checking methods, which often required manual inspection, are increasingly
replaced by AI-supported systems. These systems use advanced image-processing algorithms
to confirm the authenticity of the licence and detect possible forgeries.

## Feature-based recognition

One of the groundbreaking applications of AI in licence checking is the precise
recognition of specific features. Using neural networks, AI systems analyse information
such as document features and image data and match it against the data stored on the
licence. This not only increases identification accuracy but also speeds up the checks.

## Benefits for security and efficiency

Implementing AI in licence checks offers numerous advantages. Besides higher accuracy and
security, it enables faster processing. Automated identification supports liability
avoidance and helps minimise fraud and identity theft.

The integration of AI into driving-licence checks promises a future in which
identification becomes more precise, faster and more secure.
